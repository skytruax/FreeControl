What goes in which field of the CurseForge project form (World of Warcraft > Addons).

Project
    id 1800965, slug free-control
    https://authors.curseforge.com/#/projects/1800965/general
    public page once a moderator approves it: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/free-control

Name
    FreeControl - Controller & Gamepad Cross Hotbar

    CurseForge search matches a project's name, summary and author, not its description, and puts
    name matches first. The words after the dash are what people type. The game's name and version
    numbers stay out of it: the naming rule forbids them.

Summary
    FFXIV-style cross hotbar for controller and gamepad play in the normal interface, no Gamepad UI mode needed: hold LT, RT or both for 32 slots that take any spell, item or macro, as a light alternative to ConsolePort.

    216 characters; the field takes 256 and asks for one clear sentence. Search matches the summary,
    so it carries the words people type: controller, gamepad, cross hotbar, FFXIV, ConsolePort.
    "ConsolePort" is here because the summary is the only honest place for it: it is what puts the
    project in front of someone searching for that addon. To leave it out, end the sentence at
    "macro."

Relations
    none. FreeControl needs no other addon. A relation to an addon it merely resembles is not what
    the field is for.

Later versions
    The project id is in FreeControl/FreeControl.toc ("## X-Curse-Project-ID: 1800965"), so pushing
    a version tag uploads the new file to CurseForge by itself.
